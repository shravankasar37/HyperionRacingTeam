WHO WE ARE

Pioneering India's Racing Revolution

Hyperion Racing Team is a Combustion category Formula Student racing team from Pillai College of Engineering, competing in Formula Student competitions across India. We design, build, and race single-seater formula-style race cars powered by internal combustion engines, pushing the boundaries of automotive engineering through hands-on innovation and technical excellence.

🔥

COMBUSTION EXCELLENCE

We specialize in internal combustion engine technology, optimizing power output, fuel efficiency, and reliability through advanced engine tuning and powertrain development.

🏎️

FORMULA STUDENT COMPETITION

Competing in Formula Student India and international events, we showcase our engineering capabilities through static events (design, cost, business) and dynamic events (acceleration, skid pad, autocross, endurance).

🔧

HANDS-ON ENGINEERING

From design to fabrication, our team builds every component in-house. We learn by doing—designing, prototyping, testing, and refining our race cars through real-world application.
